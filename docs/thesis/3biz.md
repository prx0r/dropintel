## Key Validation Points

### Google Just Validated the Behaviour
At Google I/O 2026, Google announced that for categories including home repair, users can ask Search to call businesses on their behalf to get pricing and availability. Rolling out in U.S. now.

User journey changing from:
**Google → websites → forms → phone calls → quotes → customer coordinates everything**

Into:
**user → agent → providers → structured comparison → booking**

### Lokuli / BookingClaw Is Live
AI-native local-services directory covering 888 service categories in Los Angeles.
- Provider discovery
- Structured pricing
- Real-time availability
- Reviews
- MCP
- Booking
- Stripe payment
- Platform fee on successful bookings

Their architecture validates the category exists.

### Rightcharge Already Proves UK Market
UK customers already use intermediaries for EV charger → installer → installation → energy tariff.
Government structure helps: OZEV-approved installer system, grants up to £500/socket.

### Heat Pumps Are Even Better
UK Boiler Upgrade Scheme is installer-led. MCS-certified installer applies for and redeems grant.
Grants: £7,500 typical, £9,000 off-gas-grid.
Consumer already needs trusted intermediary.
