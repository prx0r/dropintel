## Why That's Not the End

### Problem 1: ChatGPT Doesn't Know Your Business

```
Customer: "I need an EV charger installed"
ChatGPT: "Here are 3 installers in Nottingham"
Customer: "Which one is OZEV approved?"
ChatGPT: "... I don't know"
```

**ChatGPT can search. It can't validate.**

It doesn't know:
- Which installer is certified
- What they charge
- When they're available
- What equipment they support
- Their completion rate
- Their insurance status

**That data doesn't exist in Shopify Catalog.**

### Problem 2: The Supplier Side Is Missing

```
CUSTOMER SIDE (ChatGPT owns this)
User → Agent → Product/Service → Checkout

SUPPLIER SIDE (nobody owns this)
Installer → Availability → Pricing → Certification → Booking → Fulfillment
```

**ChatGPT can't make the installer show up on Thursday at 2pm.**

### Problem 3: The Data Is Proprietary

```
ChatGPT knows: "Here are OZEV installers in NG7"
ChatGPT doesn't know: "Jim is available Thursday, has a Zappi cert, and charges £950"

Only Jim's AI front desk knows that.
Only our Supplier OS knows that.
```
