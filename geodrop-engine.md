## Core Doctrine

**We do not want to compete. We want to find commercial objects that SHOULD exist but do not.**

Reject categories where success depends primarily on:
- cheaper prices
- prettier branding
- generic SEO
- competing against excellent specialist merchants

**The moat is:** authoritative identity, compatibility, supersession, live supply, transaction path.
